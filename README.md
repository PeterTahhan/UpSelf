# ⚔️ Quest Master AI: Real-Life Gamification Engine

Transform your personal habits, routines, and skill-building goals into an interactive RPG system. **Quest Master AI** acts as a dynamic Game Master, translating real-world progress into experience points (XP), title evolutions, daily habit tracking, and high-reward bounty challenges across any field you choose.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📈 **Dynamic Leveling & XP:** Gain XP for completing daily tasks and high-difficulty bounties to level up overall and within specific fields.
- 🏷️ **Field-Specific Title Progression:** Earn progressive titles as you rank up (e.g., *Novice Lifter* → *Beginner Bodybuilder* → *Sculpted Athlete* → *Titan*).
- 📅 **Daily Quests & Streak Bonuses:** Track recurring daily habits with built-in multiplier bonuses for consistent streaks.
- 🎯 **Bounty Challenges:** Unique weekly objectives and high-reward "Boss Bounties" to push your limits.
- 🔄 **Adaptive Difficulty:** Automatically scales challenge difficulty based on your consistency, with burnout prevention mechanisms.
- 📜 **Structured Player Dashboard:** Visual Markdown player profiles generated right inside your chat session.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Copy the Prompt
Copy the complete prompt from [`prompt.txt`](./prompt.txt) (or the System Prompt provided in this repository).

### 2. Set Up Your Game Session
Paste the prompt into your preferred LLM interface (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or custom System Instructions) to initialize your session.

### 3. Initialize Your Character
The AI will ask you three quick setup questions:
1. **Focus Fields:** (e.g., *Bodybuilding, Software Engineering, Cybersecurity, Writing*)
2. **Daily Availability:** (e.g., *30 minutes, 1–2 hours*)
3. **Difficulty Setting:** (*Casual, Balanced, or Hardcore*)

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Check Your Quests:** The AI generates your active **Daily Quests** and **Active Bounties**.
2. **Report Progress:** Simply send a message logging what you finished:
   > *"Completed 30 minutes of weightlifting, drank 2L of water, and finished 1 LeetCode problem."*
3. **Claim XP & Rank Up:** The AI updates your profile status, awards XP, logs your achievements, and assigns new titles upon leveling up!
